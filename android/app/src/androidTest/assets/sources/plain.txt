Chapter One

The North Wind and the Sun were disputing which was the stronger, when a
traveler came along wrapped in a warm cloak. See https://example.com/fable
for the original telling[1].

[1]: Aesop, "The North Wind and the Sun", fable 46.

2

They agreed that the one who first made the traveler take off his cloak
should be considered stronger than the other. ![wind and sun](https://example.com/wind.png)

page 3

Then the North Wind blew as hard as he could; but the more he blew the more
closely did the traveler fold his cloak around him; and at last the North
Wind gave up the attempt. Then the Sun shone out warmly, and immediately
the traveler took off his cloak.

Chapter Two

And so the North Wind was obliged to confess [2] that the Sun was stronger
of the two, as [told by Aesop](https://example.com/aesop). They parted as
friends after the contest. [Image: the sun prevailing]
